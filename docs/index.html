03 / INSIDE THE LEARNED MODEL

Ordinary regression.
Visible coefficients.

No pretrained weights, neural-network badge or remote API. Original Python and JavaScript cores implement the formula, feature expansion, matrix factorization and prediction independently.

01 / SAMPLE

A closed teaching box.

Seven evenly spaced coordinates per input axis form 343 training points. Compute five analytic Greeks for each option type. No observed prices or implied-volatility calibration.

02 / NORMALIZE

Training statistics only.

Training means and population standard deviations scale all three inputs and ten outputs. Include every polynomial monomial up to total degree four, including a constant.

03 / SOLVE

Fit, do not memorize.

Solve the ridge normal equations using an original Cholesky factorization. Fixed penalty 0.01 on nonconstant coefficients. No holdout tuning or optimization claim.

04 / A SURROGATE, NOT A SIGNAL

The teacher has
assumptions too.

Black-Scholes is a model, not market truth.

European exercise, constant volatility/rates/dividend yield, lognormal diffusion and idealized frictionless trading. No smile, jumps, stochastic rates, transaction costs or early exercise. A perfect fit to the formula would not prove real-market accuracy.

Errors can grow at boundaries.

The displayed holdout lies inside training cells. It does not certify all points in the box, corners or parameters outside it. Low-order polynomial Greeks can violate financial bounds. Nothing is silently clamped or presented as a calibrated risk model.

No live finance, no hidden service.

No market data, broker keys, portfolio, trade, money or investment advice. Everything runs locally in this page, without uploads, storage, cookies or app network requests. Static hosting may log ordinary asset requests. Source links leave this page.
